Mesquite Oil Tools Product Catalog

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Mesquite Oil Tools, Inc. ("Mesquite") provides the Mesquite Oil Tools Product Catalog app (the "App"), an informational catalog. Its categories, product information, illustrations, specification tables and search index are bundled with the App and available offline.

Information we collect

The App itself does not collect, sell or share personal information, and it does not transmit information to Mesquite or an app server. It has no account or login, backend, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDK. It does not collect location data, request location or other sensitive device permissions, or use an advertising identifier.

Searches and on-device storage

Searches and navigation state are handled on your device during the active app session. The App does not transmit search terms, keep a search history, or create a profile. It does not save personal information or other user-generated data.

The catalog files are part of the installed App. They remain on the device while the App is installed and are removed as part of the operating system's app-removal process. The App does not retain personal information. Device operating systems may separately process diagnostics or backups according to your settings and their own policies; those services are not provided by the App or Mesquite.

Links, calls and information outside the App

The App makes no backend or background network requests. If you tap the company website link or phone number, the App hands that action to your device's browser or phone app. The browser, website operator, phone carrier and operating system may process information needed for the website visit or call under their own terms and privacy practices. Those external services are not controlled by this App.
